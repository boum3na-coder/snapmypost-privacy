Publisher: [PUBLISHER LEGAL NAME] Support/privacy contact: [REAL SUPPORT EMAIL OR CONTACT URL] Public policy URL: [PUBLIC HTTPS PRIVACY POLICY URL] What the extension accesses When you capture or select a post, SnapMyPost reads its rendered structure, displayed author/username, visible text, links, timestamps when explicitly available, and displayed media references. It captures the active browser tab's pixels and crops/stitches the selected region. A timestamp that is unavailable is stored as missing; a page URL used instead of a post permalink is labeled as a fallback. Video content is captured as still frames. Automatic expansion and scrolling may cause the website itself to load additional content under that site's policy. All-website access allows existing cross-site selection shortcuts, area capture and optional hover controls. It does not cause the extension to record normal browsing history. The extension does not bypass authentication, private-content restrictions, DRM or website access controls. Local processing and retention Image processing, clipboard writes, SHA-256 hashing, metadata generation and editing run locally. Captures and their titles/metadata are temporarily held in browser session storage for the editor/copy workflow. Captures expire after 30 minutes; indexed expired records are cleaned up during subsequent captures. The editor or batch-results page removes source records after reading them. A small status/index entry may remain until cleanup or the browser clears extension session storage. Screenshot history is off by default. If you enable it, original PNGs, extracted metadata, names, tags, notes and collection labels are retained in the extension's IndexedDB database until you delete them. Research mode adds original-PNG SHA-256 checksums to newly archived records. Turning history off stops new retention; it does not delete existing records. Use History → Delete/Delete all to remove saved records. Uninstalling clears extension-managed local storage, subject to browser behavior. Language, theme, capture/export preferences, shortcuts and any optional feedback URL remain in local extension storage. Downloads and copied images are controlled by your operating system and other applications and are not deleted by the extension. Clipboard history/cloud sync may retain content under your OS settings. Transmission The capture workflow contains no screenshot-upload service, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, developer telemetry, remote executable code, remote fonts or remote translation requests. No screenshots, extracted text, URLs or metadata are automatically sent to the developer. Bundled locale catalogs provide ordinary interface translations. Website resource loading remains governed by the website and browser. The optional feedback tool downloads a local JSON report containing product/version, chosen language and the message you enter. It does not automatically attach screenshots, visited URLs or browsing history. It sends nothing automatically. If you configure and open an HTTPS feedback form, the extension opens it without attaching report data; that provider may receive connection information and anything you explicitly submit. There is no default feedback provider. The publisher must identify any future default provider and its policy before shipping it. Choices and disclosure You decide when to capture, copy, download, share, archive or export metadata. A focused extension popup may briefly open when copying without the editor, because native PNG clipboard writing needs a focused document. Notifications and the copy popup have an eight-second lifetime; failures provide a retry where possible. The publisher must verify these disclosures, supply real identity/contact information and host this completed policy at a public HTTPS URL before publishing.
